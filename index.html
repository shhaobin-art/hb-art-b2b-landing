Wholesale Buyer Guide

Frequently Asked Questions for B2B Buyers

Key information on custom packaging, MOQs, sample policies, safety certifications, and international shipping.

What is your MOQ for custom OEM & private-label orders? +

We offer flexible tiered MOQs tailored to your market testing needs. Standard OEM private-label production (full custom packaging and barrel imprint) typically begins at 500 to 1,000 sets. For boutique publishers, educational startups, or initial test runs, we provide digital printing solutions with lower minimums. Standard catalog stock items can dispatch in smaller quantities.

Can you provide physical samples and custom packaging die-line templates? +

Yes, absolutely. We provide physical blank samples and color swatches for quality, lead-core smoothness, and pigment evaluation before mass production. For custom-printed tin boxes or paper cartons, our engineering team supplies vector packaging die-line templates (AI & PDF format) so your illustrators can easily lay out original artwork.

What are your lead times for sample approval and mass production? +

Custom pre-production samples are completed within 7 to 10 days. Mass production lead time is typically 25 to 35 days after artwork approval and deposit receipt. For recurring distributors with standing orders or seasonal launches, expedited scheduling is available.

Are H&B art supplies certified for US and European retail compliance? +

Yes. All H&B art supplies undergo third-party laboratory testing and strictly comply with:
• EN 71-1, 71-2, 71-3 (European Toy Safety Directive)
• ASTM D-4236 / LHAMA (US toxicological non-toxic evaluation)
• EU REACH Regulation (SVHC-free, heavy metal free)
Our manufacturing facilities hold BSCI social responsibility audits and ISO 9001 quality management certificates. Full test report copies (PDF) are provided for customs clearance and retail compliance.

What international shipping terms and logistics do you support? +

We support standard Incoterms including FOB (Shanghai / Ningbo), CIF, and door-to-door DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) by sea freight, air cargo, or express courier. With DDP, our logistics partners manage customs clearance and tariff payments, delivering goods straight to your commercial warehouse across the United States, United Kingdom, and the European Union.

What are your payment terms for wholesale and OEM contracts? +

Standard payment terms for new custom orders are 30% T/T deposit upon order confirmation, with the remaining 70% balance paid prior to dispatch or against the Bill of Lading (B/L) copy. Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C at sight) are accepted for volume container shipments.
