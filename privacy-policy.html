Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Shanghai Haobin Stationery & Art Material Co., Ltd. ("H&B Art", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://shhaobinart.com. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our wholesale customers, distributors, business partners, and website visitors in accordance with global data protection regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide B2B manufacturing, OEM/ODM quotations, and sample fulfillment services:

  • Inquiry & Contact Data: Company name, business email address, contact person, order volume estimations, product interest, and custom project specifications submitted via our wholesale quote forms.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address (anonymized), browser type, referring URL, device information, and browsing patterns collected via Google Analytics 4 to evaluate site performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your business information solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To evaluate your wholesale requirements and provide formal FOB/CIF/DDP quotations.
  • To arrange sample delivery, lab test reports (EN71 / ASTM D-4236), and packaging die-lines.
  • To execute and manage OEM/ODM purchase orders and production contracts.
  • To improve our website functionality, navigation, and product presentation.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Service Providers & Third Parties

We partner with reputable infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Formspree Inc.: Secure processing and transmission of inquiry form submissions to our corporate email servers.
  • Google Analytics 4: Aggregate statistical analysis of website traffic (IP anonymization enabled).
  • Vercel Inc. & Cloudflare Inc.: Global website hosting, SSL/TLS certificate management, and DDoS protection.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS SSL/TLS encryption, to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. We retain inquiry and transaction data only as long as necessary to fulfill the business purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory commercial accounting obligations.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to request erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") of your personal data.
  • Right to restrict or object to the processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at sally.cao@shhaobin-art.com.
