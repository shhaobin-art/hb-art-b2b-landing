Pan vs tube watercolors — what's the difference?+

Pan (cake) watercolors are compact, travel-friendly and ideal for sketch kits and beginners. Tube watercolors offer concentrated pigment for large washes and studio work. We manufacture both, plus solid sets and complete cased palettes.
